About The Arx

Company profile

Who we are

The Arx is a real estate company that helps people buy, sell and rent residential and commercial property. We combine local market knowledge with a client-first approach to make every transaction clear, safe and rewarding.

Our name reflects what we stand for: a strong, dependable foundation. Whether you are buying your first home, renting an apartment, acquiring land or investing in commercial space, we are with you at every step.

Company background

We started with a simple observation: finding a good property should not be confusing or stressful. By focusing on verified listings, honest communication and attentive service, we have built a reputation as a company clients are happy to recommend.
